ROBOTIC DOG FEATURES

What can our Robot Dog do?

Voice Control

Control the robotic dog using predefined voice commands through the SU-03T voice recognition module.

Live Camera Monitoring

ESP32-CAM provides real-time video monitoring through a wireless network.

Obstacle Detection

The ultrasonic sensor detects obstacles and helps the robot avoid collisions.

Smart Movement

Servo motors control the movement of the four legs, allowing the robot to walk and turn.

The Technology Behind the Robot Dog

ESP32

The brain of the robotic dog , controlling sensors, motors, and smart functions.

ESP32-CAM

A complete camera module for ral-time video streaming and remote monitoring.

Ultrasonic Sensor

Detect obstacles and measures distance for safe and smart navigation.

PCA9685

Control multiple servo motors for precise and coordinated movement.

MG90s

Provides precise and reliable movement for the robotic dog's joints.

SU-03T

Enables voice commands for smart and hands-free robot control.

XL-4016

Provides stable and adjustable power to the robotic dog's electronic components.

LM2596

Converts voltage efficiently to provide stable power for electronic components.
